Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I clean my solar panels in Florida?+

More often than most people expect. Florida's humidity causes pollen to bond to panel glass instead of washing off, and bird activity is year-round here. We typically recommend every 3–6 months, quarterly if you want to stay ahead of it.

Will cleaning actually improve my energy output?+

In almost every case, yes. Soiled panels can lose up to 30% of their output, and it happens gradually, so most people don't notice until it's significant. Most of our customers see a jump in their solar app within a few days.

Do you use any chemicals on my panels?+

Never. We use only ultra-pure water (0 dissolved solids) and soft brushes made for solar panel glass. No soap, no detergents, nothing that could leave residue or void your manufacturer warranty.

What areas do you serve?+

We're based in Orlando and cover greater Central Florida — Kissimmee, Sanford, Winter Park, Oviedo, Lake Mary, Clermont, Altamonte Springs, and surrounding areas. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask us.

How long does a cleaning take?+

Most homes take 45 minutes to about 2 hours depending on panel count and roof access. We're in and out, you don't need to be home or rearrange your schedule for us.

Do you clean panels in the rain?+

We want every job to be done right, so we reserve the right to reschedule in the event of rain or any unfavorable weather conditions for solar panel or window cleaning. If weather affects your appointment we'll reach out as soon as possible to get you rebooked at the next available time.
