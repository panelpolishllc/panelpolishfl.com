Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I clean my solar panels in Florida? +

Florida's humidity, pollen, afternoon storms, and bird activity mean panels get dirty fast. We recommend cleaning every 3–6 months to keep output at its peak year-round.

Will cleaning actually improve my energy output? +

Yes — dirty solar panels can lose up to 30% of their efficiency. Most customers notice a measurable improvement on their energy bill within the first billing cycle after a professional clean.

Do you use any chemicals on my panels? +

No harsh chemicals — ever. We use a pure water system and soft brushes that safely remove dirt, pollen, and grime without voiding your panel warranty or leaving any residue.

What areas do you serve? +

We serve Orlando and the greater Central Florida area, including Kissimmee, Sanford, Winter Park, Oviedo, Lake Mary, Clermont, Altamonte Springs, and surrounding communities.

How long does a cleaning take? +

Most residential jobs take 45 minutes to 2 hours depending on panel count and roof accessibility. We work efficiently and cleanly so you can get back to your day.
