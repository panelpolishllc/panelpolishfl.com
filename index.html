The Hidden Risk

What Happens When Florida
Panels Go Uncleaned

Most homeowners don't realize how much Florida's climate affects their panels. The good news is it's an easy fix. Here's what to know.

🔥

Hot Spots

Dirt and bird droppings block individual cells, causing them to overheat instead of generate power. Hot spots reduce efficiency by up to 15%, cause irreversible cell damage, and are one of the leading causes of panel failure and fire risk.

🌿

Florida Pollen Bonding

Florida humidity causes pollen to bond and harden onto panel glass. Ordinary rainfall cannot remove it. This semi-adhesive layer quietly blocks 10–25% of your sunlight every month it goes untreated.

📉

Silent Efficiency Loss

Dirty panels don't trigger alerts. Your system looks fine while output quietly drops. A 15% soiling loss on a typical system costs $300+ per year. Most homeowners don't notice until they're looking at 18 months of declining data.

📋

Warranty at Risk

Some manufacturers require professional cleaning every 6 months and documented proof before honoring a claim. Without a maintenance record, they can deny your claim entirely. Even on a system that's only a few years old.

Central Florida's Climate Demands More

The national recommendation of once per year is not enough for Central Florida. For homes in Orange, Lake, Seminole, and Polk counties, experts recommend 2–4 cleanings per year based on local conditions:

☀ Spring pollen season coats panels in polymerizing oak pollen
⛈ Summer storms deposit mineral-rich debris that dries hard
💧 High humidity causes pollen and dust to cake rather than blow off
🦅 Year-round bird activity deposits acidic droppings that etch glass

Don't wait until the damage is done. Professional cleaning protects your output, your warranty, and your investment.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I clean my solar panels in Florida? +

More often than most people expect. Florida’s humidity causes pollen to bond to panel glass instead of washing off, and bird activity is year-round here. We typically recommend every 3–6 months, quarterly if you want to stay ahead of it.

Will cleaning actually improve my energy output? +

In almost every case, yes. Soiled panels can lose up to 30% of their output, and it happens gradually, so most people don’t notice until it’s significant. Most of our customers see a jump in their solar app within a few days.

Do you use any chemicals on my panels? +

Never. We use only ultra-pure water (0 dissolved solids) and soft brushes made for solar panel glass. No soap, no detergents, nothing that could leave residue or void your manufacturer warranty.

What areas do you serve? +

We’re based in Orlando and cover greater Central Florida: Kissimmee, Sanford, Winter Park, Oviedo, Lake Mary, Clermont, Altamonte Springs, and surrounding areas. Not sure if you’re in range? Just ask us.

How long does a cleaning take? +

Most homes take 45 minutes to about 2 hours depending on panel count and roof access. We’re in and out, you don’t need to be home or rearrange your schedule for us.
