Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I clean my solar panels in Florida?
More often than most people expect. Florida's humidity causes pollen and grime to bond to panel glass and harden over time. Left untreated, this causes hot spots, accelerated wear, and potential warranty issues. We recommend every 6–12 months at minimum.
Will cleaning actually improve my energy output?
Yes. Beyond performance, dirty panels can develop hot spots that cause permanent cell damage over time. Regular cleaning protects your panels from the kind of buildup that shortens their lifespan and risks voiding your warranty.
Do you use any chemicals on my panels?
Never. We use only ultra-pure water (0 dissolved solids) and soft brushes made for solar panel glass. No soap, no detergents, nothing that could leave residue or void your manufacturer warranty.
What areas do you serve?
We're based in Orlando and cover greater Central Florida, including Kissimmee, Sanford, Winter Park, Oviedo, Lake Mary, Clermont, Altamonte Springs, and surrounding areas. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask us.
How long does a cleaning take?
Most homes take 45 minutes to about 2 hours depending on panel count and roof access. We're in and out, you don't need to be home or rearrange your schedule for us.
Do you clean panels in the rain?
We want every job to be done right, so we reserve the right to reschedule in the event of rain or any unfavorable weather conditions. If weather affects your appointment we'll reach out as soon as possible to get you rebooked at the next available time.
