Know the Difference

Does My System Need a Cleaning or Restoration?

Here’s how to tell what your panels are dealing with - and which service is right for you.

Cleaning Needed

Dust, Pollen & Light Debris

When panels look dull or dusty, a standard pure-water wash removes buildup and restores output. Most panels fall into this category.

Restoration Needed

Heavy Staining, Oxidation & Grime

Hard water deposits, bird droppings, and oxidation require a deeper restoration treatment to safely remove buildup and recover lost efficiency.

What We Offer

Our Services

Whether you need a one-time clean or want someone to take care of your panels on a regular basis, we've got you covered.

🏠

Residential Cleaning

Our pure water system (0 dissolved solids) lifts dirt, pollen, and buildup with no chemicals and no mineral residue. Dries spotless every time.

💪

Solar Panel Restoration

Heavy-duty treatment for panels with stubborn mold, bird droppings, and hardened grime. Removes buildup that causes hot spots and permanent cell damage if left untreated.

✨

Window Restoration

Full restoration for windows with hard water stains, mineral deposits, oxidation, or heavy buildup that standard cleaning can't remove. Leaves glass crystal clear.

🔍

Panel Inspection

We inspect for cracks, hot spots, bird damage, and debris build-up.

🪟

Exterior Window Cleaning

Crystal clear exterior windows using our pure water system. No streaks, no chemicals, no ladders scratching your frames. Starting at $149 for up to 15 windows.

📅

Maintenance Plans

Annual or bi-annual recurring plans to protect your panels, preserve your warranty documentation, and extend the life of your system. We handle the scheduling.
